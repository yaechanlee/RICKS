Provisional rule-based screening uses titles and abstracts. Korea must be explicit in the title or linked to a research setting or substantive topic in the abstract. Uncertain cases are kept for review; classifications can contain errors. Articles and reviews may still include trade publications: source types were not supplied in the CSV.

Node size = papers with that country’s affiliations. Hong Kong and Macau are shown separately as reported in affiliation metadata.

검색 및 집계 방법

Each document counts once per country and once per distinct country pair. Three-country papers contribute to all three pairs. Single-author papers contribute to country totals but no collaboration edges. Missing author or affiliation data are flagged. Multiple affiliations of one author can affect country interpretation. This is an affiliation co-occurrence proxy for country coauthorship.
