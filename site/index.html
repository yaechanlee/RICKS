OUR PURPOSE

A place for careful research
and public conversation.

The Hanyang Research Institute for Contemporary Korean Studies is a scholarly community devoted to the politics, society, culture, and international relations of contemporary Korea. We bring researchers and students with different perspectives together to deepen understanding of Korean society and connect academic work with public discussion.
