ABOUT THE INSTITUTE

An open scholarly community
for the study of Korea

The Hanyang Research Institute for Contemporary Korean Studies is a scholarly community devoted to the politics, society, culture, and international relations of contemporary Korea. We bring researchers and students with different perspectives together to deepen understanding of Korean society and connect academic work with public discussion.

Our mission and activities ↗
01

Rigorous research

We examine the central issues of contemporary Korea through historical and comparative perspectives.

02

Support for emerging scholars

We create opportunities for graduate students to develop their research and public writing.

03

Public conversation

We share research in accessible formats and contribute to wider debates about Korea.
